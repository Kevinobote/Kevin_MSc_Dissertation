\section*{Abstract}
This study presents an end-to-end Kiswahili audio processing pipeline integrating automatic speech recognition (ASR), sentiment analysis, and text summarization, addressing the challenges of low-resource and multilingual settings. Using the Swahili subset of the Mozilla Common Voice dataset, we developed and evaluated models for each task: a fine-tuned Wav2Vec2 ASR model, a multilingual DistilBERT model for sentiment classification, and a T5-based model for text summarization.
Our ASR model achieved a Word Error Rate (WER) of 0.2917 on the test set, with an average sample WER of 0.1914, indicating approximately 81\% word-level accuracy. Sentiment analysis revealed significant class imbalance, with the model exhibiting high confidence for neutral predictions but low performance on positive and negative classes, highlighting the need for culturally-aware, Swahili-specific sentiment datasets. Summarization experiments using mT5-multilingual-XLSum outperformed baseline models, achieving ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L scores of 0.4596, 0.3211, and 0.424 respectively, demonstrating the feasibility of generating concise summaries from ASR transcripts without relying on extensive groundtruth annotations.
We also conducted qualitative and quantitative error analyses, identifying dataset biases towards young male speakers and the limitations of cross-lingual sentiment transfer, which may affect the fairness and generalizability of the system. To address these issues, we implemented modular chunking strategies for both audio and text, asynchronous processing pipelines, and comprehensive aggregation mechanisms to ensure scalability and robustness.
Finally, we deployed the system as a web-based prototype using FastAPI and a responsive frontend, providing a practical demonstration of the pipeline’s end-to-end capabilities. This research contributes a reproducible, research-grade pipeline for Kiswahili language processing, establishes benchmarks for low-resource multilingual NLP tasks, and underscores the importance of ethical considerations, dataset diversity, and culturally-aware modeling in AI system development.
